Before handing over a job

The questions we are asked, answered in full.

Prices, timing, payment, confidentiality and the limits of what we do. If something is missing, just ask: the answer will be as precise as these.

Do you really write in Chinese and Arabic?

Yes, and the method is stated openly, rather than hidden. The text is built here: the structure, the argument, the figures a technical buyer looks for. Then, before delivery, it is read and corrected by a native professional. That review is included in the price, and it is why Chinese and Arabic cost 50% more and take five days longer.

Chinese is Simplified for the mainland, unless your market is Taiwan or Hong Kong. Arabic is Modern Standard Arabic, and pages are delivered right-to-left, laid out properly.

What does it cost?

Prices are whole and they are on this site: first reading of your Italian pages €39, product sheet €140, company page €180, catalogue of up to fifteen products €380, trade-fair package €690. Website pages rewritten in Italian €349; a site built from scratch €790.

No tailored quotes: the price is the price, and you know it before a word is written. The full list.

How do we start?

With an email. If you already have Italian pages online, the natural first step is the first reading: ten observations on what is already published, within 48 hours, for €39. That €39 comes off the first job in full, if you order within sixty days. It is not a discount, it is a deposit.

What do you need from us?

What you already have: the source text in your language, datasheets, product figures, certifications, photographs. It does not need to be tidy.

If something is missing — a measurement, a lead time, a standard — we ask once, in a single list, instead of coming back every day with one more question.

How long does it take?

First reading 48 hours. Product sheet and company page five days. Catalogue ten days. A website, three weeks.

Days are working days and they count from when the material arrives, not from the order: it is the only way to name a date and keep it.

Is this translation?

No, and the difference is the whole point. A translation carries the same sentences into another language; here the text is rewritten for the reader who has to decide.

An Italian buyer reads a page that came from your English one and can tell within a line that it was not written for him. Correct and useless at the same time.

Who actually writes?

One person, always the same one, in Modena. There is no newsroom behind this and the work is not passed on to anyone else.

Automatic tools are used to check, not to write: what reaches you was thought through and read back by the person who hands it over.

What do you not write?

Machine instruction manuals, food labels, declarations of conformity, safety data sheets. That is regulated material: the law decides the language and responsibility stays with the manufacturer.

We say so before taking the job, not after.

How is it paid, and what do we receive?

On delivery, by bank transfer. Every job comes with a proper Italian receipt for occasional work (prestazione occasionale), which your accounts department can book normally.

Before anything starts, what is included and by what date is agreed in writing.

Why no Italian VAT number?

Because the activity is occasional under article 2222 of the Italian Civil Code, and an Italian VAT number depends on the work being habitual, not on turnover.

For you nothing changes in substance: you receive a valid document. What changes is that no open-ended commitments are taken on — a monthly maintenance contract, for instance.

What if the text is not right?

One round of revision is included in every job, and that is what it is for: changing what does not work, not rewriting from scratch.

If after the revision the work is still not what you needed, we say so plainly and do not press: better to lose a job than deliver a page nobody will use.

Do our documents stay confidential?

Yes. Price lists, drawings, datasheets and figures you send are used to write and go nowhere else.

No client work is published as a sample without written consent: the example documents on this site are built on invented companies, deliberately.

Do you work for agencies?

Yes, white label: we write, the work goes out under your name, and we do not speak to your client.

The price list stays the same, so you can quote without having to ask anyone.

Do you build the website too?

The pages of a site that already exists, rewritten in Italian and delivered ready to upload, or the site built from scratch for a company that has none.

No online shops, no booking systems, no panels to keep updated, no monthly maintenance: if you need one of those we say so at once and point you to someone who does it. The service page.
