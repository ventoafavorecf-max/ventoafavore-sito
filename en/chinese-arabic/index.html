Why it matters

The language counts after the fair, when the sheet is read again.

At a trade fair in the Gulf everyone speaks English: it is the language of appointments and of the first round of questions. What matters comes later.

The sheet left on the table is read again in an office, days later, by someone who was not at the fair. Often an engineer, who reads English more slowly than the person who collected it. At that moment two identical sheets are no longer identical: one is in the reader’s own language, the other is not.

It is true of the Gulf and it is true of China. And it is the opposite of what is usually assumed: the language is not there for the negotiation. It is there for the moment when nobody from your company is present to explain.

The limits

Two things Vento a Favore does not do, stated in advance

  • No regulated documents, in any language

    Instruction manuals, food labels, declarations of conformity, safety data sheets. The wording is set by regulation and the responsibility remains the manufacturer’s. In Chinese and Arabic the rule applies twice over.

  • No hosting of a site inside China

    A site hosted outside China opens slowly there, and hosting it inside the country requires registration with the Chinese authorities, which Vento a Favore does not hold. The files are delivered finished; the hosting is best chosen by someone who knows that market.
