What the first reading is not

Said now, so that nobody is disappointed later.

  • It is not the job

    Ten observations show the approach. They do not replace the rewriting of a website or a catalogue.

  • It is not a school report

    The Italian pages of a foreign company are often produced in a hurry and on a small budget. That is common, and it is nobody’s fault.

  • It does not cover regulated documents

    Instruction manuals, food labels, declarations of conformity and safety data sheets are excluded: their wording is set by regulation.

  • It commits you to nothing

    Once the reading is delivered, you have a useful document even if you decide to do the rest in-house. There will be no follow-up calls.
