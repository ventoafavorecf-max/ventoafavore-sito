Ten observations, in two days, on your Italian pages.

Before having something written by somebody you do not know, it is reasonable to want a small test first. Honesty

What the first reading is not

Said in advance, so that nobody is disappointed afterwards.

It is not the job

Ten observations show you the approach; they do not replace the rewriting of a website or a catalogue.

It is not a school report

The Italian pages of a company selling abroad were almost always done fast and on a small budget: that is the norm of the trade, not anybody’s fault.

It does not cover regulated documents

Labels, instructions for use, safety manuals and declarations of conformity stay outside: they are technical and regulatory matter, not commercial.

It commits you to nothing

Once the first reading is done you have a useful document even if you decide to do everything in-house. There will be no chasing.
