How to read them

Finished documents, not extracts

Each file is shown exactly as it is delivered: laid out, with headings, tables and notes in place. What changes from one company to the next is the data inside, and the data is yours.

The examples are built on invented companies, on purpose. No work done for a client is published without the client’s written consent.

The format

Every document comes as a typeset PDF and as editable text: you can pick it up and update it without depending on anyone.

What each one does

Six documents, six different readers

Each document is written for the person who has to use it: a distributor, a purchasing office, a shop customer, a visitor to your stand.

Ask for a document →

  1. 01

    Product sheet · €140

    For the distributor who asks for it in the first email. A few lines of verifiable facts at the top; below, sizes, packing data, certifications, delivery times and Incoterms, ready to copy into a price list. One A4 page, 5 days. You send the sheet or the label you have today.

  2. 02

    Technical data sheet · €140

    For a machine or a component, the purchasing office does not want a story: it wants values to set beside another supplier’s. Specifications, curves, versions and delivery times, in the order they are checked. 5 days. You send the current sheet and the data.

  3. 03

    Company profile · €180

    A buyer reads it to find out who you are before asking for a price. The adjectives give way to four things: how long you have been in business, in which plant, with which checks, and what you do that others do not. Text ready for the website, 5 days. You send the page as it stands.

  4. 04

    E-commerce descriptions · €380

    In a direct sale nobody is there to explain: the description is the only salesperson present. Up to fifteen products following one pattern, delivered as CSV or as text to paste. 10 days. You send the product list.

  5. 05

    Trade fair package · €540

    A company profile that says who you are and three sheets that say what you sell, written together in the same order and the same words, delivered on the same day. Ordered one at a time, they would cost €600 and take twenty working days; together, ten.

  6. 06

    Five product sheets · €550

    Five sheets on one layout, eight days instead of twenty-five. The same sheet in a second language (English, German, French or Spanish) costs €84 when ordered with the first and takes 3 days.
