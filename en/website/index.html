The problem

When the problem is not the wording, it is the page

It is a common story: a company that does good work, and an Italian page produced by translation software, or eight years out of date, or no Italian page at all.

A buyer in Italy who looks you up finds that page anyway, and at that moment it is all they know about you.

So Vento a Favore works in two ways: the pages you already have, rewritten in Italian and delivered ready to upload, or the Italian site built from scratch when there is nothing to start from.

What you get

Pages, not a system

Whether it is your existing pages or a new site, four rules apply.

  1. 01

    Nothing to maintain

    No admin panel, no passwords, no plug-in that stops working one morning. The pages stay exactly as delivered until Vento a Favore changes them at your request.

  2. 02

    Figures before the story

    The information comes in the order a buyer needs it to decide: the standard, the dimensions, the lead time, who answers the phone. The company history follows, where it does its work.

  3. 03

    The domain stays yours

    Registered in your name. Vento a Favore pays for it up front and passes on the cost, with no mark-up. The hosting is free and in your name too.

  4. 04

    Fast, and it stays fast

    No libraries to download, nothing that tracks the reader: the pages are light and open quickly even on a factory-floor connection.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises

The price is the one on the list, and you know it before you get in touch. The clock starts at the third step, not the first.

  1. To begin

    The request

    One email with the address of your current site, if there is one, or two lines on what you make. No compulsory form.

  2. Within a day

    The proposal

    How many pages are needed, what goes into them, the price from the list and the working days.

  3. Then

    The material

    The texts you have, photographs if there are any, the product data. The count of days starts here. If there are no usable photographs, you are told in advance: the site is then built on text, figures and drawings.

  4. On delivery

    Delivery

    The site online, the domain in your name, and one round of corrections within thirty days.

The limits

What stays outside

Some of these need looking after every day; others follow rules that do not depend on the writer. Vento a Favore does not take them on, and says so before accepting the job. If you need one of them, you are told straight away, with a pointer to someone who does.

  • Online shops

    No carts, payments or stock management on the site. Product descriptions for a shop that already exists, yes: e-commerce descriptions.

  • Booking systems and members’ areas

    No calendars to manage, no password-protected areas for clients or dealers.

  • Maintenance contracts

    No monthly fee and no panel to keep updated: the pages stay as they were delivered.

  • Regulated documents

    Instruction manuals, food labels, declarations of conformity: there the wording is set by regulation and the responsibility remains the manufacturer’s.
