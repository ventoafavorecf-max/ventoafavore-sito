The website

The Italian site a buyer can actually use.

Small, real websites: a few pages, the figures at the top, nothing to update and nothing that breaks on its own. They open instantly and read properly on a phone.

What you get

Pages, not a system

Either your existing pages rewritten in Italian and handed over ready to upload, or the Italian site built from scratch when there is nothing to start from.

Nothing to maintain

No panel, no passwords, no plugins that stop working one morning. The pages stay exactly as they are until we change them.

Figures before story

The order of the information is the one a buyer needs: the standard, the dimensions, the lead time, who answers the telephone. The company history comes after, where it does its work.

The domain stays yours

Registered in your name. We pay for it and re-bill it at cost, with no mark-up. The hosting is free and in your name too.

Fast, and it stays fast

No libraries to download, nothing that follows the reader around: the pages are light and open even on the connection of a factory floor.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises

1
The request

One email with the address of your current site, or two lines about what you make. No form to fill in.

2
The proposal

Within a day: how many pages are needed, what goes into them, the price from the list and the number of working days.

3
The material

Whatever texts you have, photographs if there are any, the product data. The count starts here. If there are no usable photographs we say so before starting: the site is then built on text, figures and drawing.

4
Delivery

The site online, the domain in your name, and one round of corrections within thirty days.

The limits

What we do not do

No online shops, no booking systems, no members’ areas, no maintenance contract. Those need looking after every day, and it is not what we do best: better said now than after the quotation has been accepted.

Regulated documents stay outside as well — instruction manuals, food labels, declarations of conformity: there the language is set by the regulation and the responsibility remains the manufacturer’s.
