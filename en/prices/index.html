The rules

Few of them, written in advance

  1. 01

    Days start from the material

    They are working days, counted from the moment the material arrives, not from the order: if the label and the data arrive on a Monday, the sheet is ready by the following Monday.

  2. 02

    The €39 is deducted

    The cost of the first reading comes off in full from the first job commissioned within sixty days. Starting there means paying once, not twice.

  3. 03

    One round of corrections

    Included in every job, documents and websites alike. You ask for it within thirty days of delivery.

  4. 04

    Everything in writing

    Before work starts, what is included and the delivery date are agreed in writing.

  5. 05

    Payment

    Texts and documents: on delivery, by bank transfer. Websites: half when the work starts, half on delivery. A regular receipt is issued for every job.
