The problem, concretely

The difference shows up on a single sentence

On the left, the machine rendering into Italian — the one you can read today on a great many pages. On the right, the same information written the way somebody who has to buy in Italy looks for it.

Machinery manufacturer · button on the product page Enquire now
Machine

Indagare ora

Rewritten

Richiedi un’offerta

Indagare is what a police officer does: the machine read «enquire» as «investigate». It sits on the one button that turns a visitor into a contact, and an Italian buyer reads it as a page nobody ever checked. Richiedi un’offerta — ask for a quotation — is what every Italian manufacturer writes there.

Cosmetics manufacturer · product page Premium bio-cellulose masks with a skin-adaptive, ultra-fine matrix structure.
Machine

Maschere premium in bio-cellulosa con una struttura a matrice ultra-fine adattiva alla pelle.

Rewritten

Maschera in biocellulosa: la trama aderisce al viso senza staccarsi, anche sul contorno occhi. Posa 15 minuti, monouso, confezione da 5.

The first line is correct Italian and tells an Italian beautician neither how the mask is used nor how long it stays on. The second one says: the mesh grips the face without lifting, even around the eyes; fifteen minutes; single use; box of five. That is what she needs in order to resell it.

Industrial supplier · contact page Monday – Thursday, 8:00 – 17:00
Machine

Lunedi – Giovedi, 8:00 – 17:00

Rewritten

Da lunedì a giovedì, 8:00 – 17:00. Assistenza in italiano: chiedete del referente per l’Italia.

Two missing accents — lunedì, giovedì — and the page tells every Italian reader that no Italian has ever read it. Nobody will write to point it out. The rewritten line also answers the question a buyer of industrial equipment asks first: who picks up the phone, and in which language.
