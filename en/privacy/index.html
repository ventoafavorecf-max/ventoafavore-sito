Privacy and cookies

Which data is needed, and where it goes

The site uses no cookies and does not measure visits. Data arrives only if you write, and it is used only to reply to you.

Cookies
none
Visit tracking
none
Deletion
on simple request

Who handles the data

The data controller is Vento a Favore, based in Modena, Italy. For any request about your data: ventoafavorecf@gmail.com.

What the site collects

Browsing the site does not require leaving any data. The site installs no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools, embeds no videos or maps from others and loads its fonts and images from its own servers. For this reason there is no cookie banner: consent is not needed for something that does not exist.

As with any website, the service that hosts it, GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., part of the Microsoft group), records the technical data of each visit, including the IP address, for security and operation. Vento a Favore has no access to it.

If you write

The contact form asks for name, company and email; the telephone number is optional. The feedback form asks for the company; the name is optional. The text of the message is up to you. The form sends the data to a Google service (Google Apps Script) that delivers it to the Vento a Favore mailbox; if you prefer, you can write directly to the mailbox or on WhatsApp.

The data is used to answer the request and, if a job follows, to carry it out and issue the receipt. The legal basis is the request itself and, for the job, the contract (Article 6(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). The data is neither sold nor added to anyone else’s lists.

The comment in the feedback form appears on the site only if you tick the box, and only with the company name.

Providing the form data is optional, but without name, company and email it is not possible to reply. No decision is made automatically and there is no profiling.

Who can see it

Google (mail and forms) and, if you use WhatsApp, Meta. They serve only to deliver and store messages. Google and Meta may process data in the United States, with the safeguards provided by the Regulation (for participating companies, the Data Privacy Framework).

How long

Messages with no follow-up are deleted when they are no longer needed to reply. For completed jobs, tax documents are kept for as long as the law requires. In any case the data is deleted on your request, unless the law requires it to be kept.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, to receive it in a readable format and to object to its processing (Articles 15-21 of the Regulation). An email to the address above is enough. If you believe the data is being handled improperly, you can contact the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Emails to companies: the full notice

This part concerns those who receive an email from Vento a Favore without having asked for it (Article 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

Who writes. Vento a Favore, Modena, Italy, ventoafavorecf@gmail.com.

Where the data comes from. From the company’s public website or a public trade-fair catalogue, where the address is published so that the company can be contacted. Every email states the source.

Which data. The company name, a contact email address, and the texts published on the website that the email comments on. No sensitive data, no private data.

Why. To offer the company a text-writing job. The stated legal basis is the legitimate interest in offering one’s work to businesses (Article 6(1)(f)), with the right to object at any time.

Objection. It is enough to reply “no thank you”: the address is removed at once and added to a list of exclusions, so that it receives no further emails. You can also ask to see or delete the data (Articles 15-21) and contact the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

How long. The address is kept for as long as this contact needs and, after a refusal, only in the exclusion list, so as not to write again.

Who sees it. Google (mail and forms). No one else.
