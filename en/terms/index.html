Terms of work

What is agreed, before starting

The same rules apply to every job: price and date in writing first, payment by bank transfer, one round of corrections included.

Before starting

For every job, what is included, the price and the delivery date are agreed in writing. Prices are those of the price list; for word-by-word reviewed translation and for tailor-made work the total is written before starting.

The days

The days stated are working days and count from when the materials arrive, not from the order. For Chinese and Arabic the price is increased by fifty per cent and the time is five days longer: the expert review is included.

The corrections

Every job includes one round of corrections, to be requested within thirty days of delivery. After that, each change costs €60.

The first reading

The first reading costs €39 and is deducted in full from the first job commissioned within sixty days.

Payment

Texts and documents are paid on delivery, by bank transfer. Websites are paid in two instalments: half at the start and half on delivery. A regular receipt is issued for every job.

The website, the domain and the hosting

The domain is registered in the company’s name. Vento a Favore advances the cost and charges it back at cost, with no mark-up. The hosting where the website lives is free of charge and in the company’s name.

What is not done

Vento a Favore does not promise sales or ranking results: it writes texts and websites, and says so beforehand.

Personal data

How the data of those who write is handled is explained on the page Privacy and cookies.
