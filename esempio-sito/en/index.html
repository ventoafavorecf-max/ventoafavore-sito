HPU-20/250-C

The power unit Vianova builds most often: a twenty-litre tank, two hundred and fifty bar maximum pressure and a four-kilowatt motor. It fits in a half-metre bay and weighs about as much as two bags of cement.

The drawing first

The design is approved and dimensioned before any metal is touched. Changes are made in the drawing office, where they cost little, not on the shop floor.

Every unit runs on the test bench for twenty minutes at rated pressure. The numbered test certificate ships with the unit.
